# Release audit — 0.15.0

October 15, 2026. The presentation and contract work is implemented. The migration analysis is **not a completed diagnostic-quality validation**.

## Implemented behavior

New managed findings require a Claude-authored short name, takeaway, local events, decisive source identities and specific limitations. Turn rows show the saved local event titles. The canonical finding starts with the answer and sequence; classifications and complete record inventories are details. Pattern episodes have separate outcomes and source membership. New saves reject unread references, invented episode keys, unrelated source membership, invalid turn anchors and reversed event ordering. These are prerequisites, not proof of semantic support.

Historical findings remain unedited. A legacy turn link opens explained claim evidence in saved order, exposes the missing local explanation, and retains the complete finding. The real historical F1 opens its crash output ahead of the background acknowledgement. No renderer-authored F2 split or shortened conclusion was created.

The updated investigation/review skills distinguish a material observation from a persistent improvement, require later-goal searches before claiming non-recovery, and review the new event explanations. Missing managed reviews now say **not saved** beside the record. Stopped runs expose their latest saved conclusion draft as **not finalized**; it does not replace the absent final conclusion.

## Remaining quality gaps

- The focused migration output is unfinished. Its reviewer says the saved “full-suite” scope and requirement map need correction. The earlier narrower passing run is absent from its saved local sequence. The historical F2 was not replaced by a fully reviewed set of distinct lookup episodes.
- The completed fixture still uses the misleading short name “Unrun filesystem test” for a test that ran and failed, then was not rerun. It repeats the unknown-cause caveat. Its takeaway citation alone does not establish every clause, although the sequence contains the relevant records. These words were preserved; a supported model-review verdict did not eliminate them.
- The first migration run's negative result remains part of the audit. A larger budget may allow review to finish, but it would not by itself establish sound grouping, concise writing or correct intent interpretation.
- A review can miss contrary evidence or approve weak wording. Deterministic validation and a writing skill do not make this free of AI slop. Independent unseen-session evaluation remains required before a superiority claim over `/insights`.
